# Research Brief

Use this before web research.

## Questions
- Who uses this?
- What do they do repeatedly?
- Is this surface primarily Operate, Read, Persuade, or Experience?
- Is the product high-frequency, high-stakes, data-dense, consumer, editorial, or promotional?
- What must feel trustworthy?
- What may feel expressive?
- What existing brand constraints are non-negotiable?
- What is the one visual quality the current UI lacks?

## Reference selection
Prefer references that solve the same problem:
- similar density;
- similar data complexity;
- similar interaction model;
- similar audience;
- similar emotional requirement.

Avoid selecting references only because they are popular.
