# Aesthetic Dimensions

Choose intentionally along these axes.

- Quiet ↔ expressive
- Dense ↔ spacious
- Editorial ↔ utilitarian
- Soft ↔ technical
- Warm ↔ cool
- Familiar ↔ experimental
- Flat ↔ layered
- Geometric ↔ organic
- Static ↔ kinetic
- Data-first ↔ image-first

Write the chosen position on at least four axes before large greenfield work.

Possible directions include:
- industrial/utilitarian;
- editorial;
- luxury/refined;
- playful;
- technical/engineering;
- archival;
- organic;
- neo-brutalist;
- geometric modernist;
- retro-futurist;
- soft tactile.

Do not mix incompatible directions unless the contrast itself is part of the concept.
